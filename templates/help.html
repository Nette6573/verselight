GETTING STARTED

How can VerseLight help?

VerseLight gives you several simple ways to explore Scripture.

📖 Random VerseReceive a Scripture for reflection and encouragement.
🔎 SearchSearch the Bible dataset using a word or phrase.
💬 Ask a QuestionAsk a Bible-related question and receive related Scriptures.
✨ GenerateRequest an original Bible-inspired passage. It is AI-generated and should never be mistaken for Scripture.
💾 SaveSave a displayed verse for the current session.

A note about AI

VerseLight uses AI to assist with topic interpretation and original Bible-inspired writing. AI-generated material is not Scripture and should not be presented as an actual Bible verse or citation.
